# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1)** to
foster an open, welcoming, and respectful community for everyone contributing
to `coree`.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming, respectful, and inclusive language.
- Provide and gracefully accept constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the software.
- Show empathy and patience toward fellow contributors and users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Public or private intimidation, doxxing, or publishing private information
  without consent.
- Submitting knowingly plagiarized, proprietary, or malicious code or assets.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues,
and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported to
the project maintainers via the repository issue tracker or GitHub repository
maintainers at `https://github.com/ahrrfy/coree`. All reports will be
reviewed fairly and confidentially.
